Automated Preprocessing Pipeline (Module 3 Specification)

Every imported record passes through the academic preprocessing flow

1. Deduplication

Drops duplicate timestamps to prevent biased weighting in regression and forecasting models.

2. Missing Value Imputation

Numeric variables (like temperature) are imputed with median estimators or forward-filled.

3. Outlier & Sanity Filtering

Negative energy values and malformed timestamps are filtered out prior to training and display.

4. Feature Extraction

Extracts calendar features: Hour, Day, DayOfWeek, Month, IsWeekend, and UsagePeriod.
